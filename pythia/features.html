Features

The dream journal is the core. Everything else sits around it.

Dream journal

Write a dream the moment you wake up, while it is still there. You can type it or dictate it out loud — most people find speaking easier at 6am than typing.

Every dream stays in your journal with its date. You can save one without interpreting it and come back later, which is useful when you remember a fragment but have no time to think about it.

AI interpretation, four ways

Each dream can be read through four different lenses:

  • Classic — traditional symbolic reading
  • Carl Jung — archetypes, the shadow, individuation
  • Sigmund Freud — latent content, wish fulfilment, displacement
  • Religious and spiritual — a faith-oriented interpretation

The interpretation is generated from the text you wrote, so two people who both dreamt about water will get very different readings.

Pattern analysis

This is the part that a single reading cannot give you. Once several dreams are saved, Pythia analyses them together and reports:

  • Recurring symbols — images that keep appearing without you noticing
  • Emotional trend — whether your dreams have been getting heavier or lighter over time
  • Underlying theme — the thread running through the set
  • One practical insight — something to take into waking life

This reads your own history. It is not a generic report.

Daily draw

Each morning there is a single card, a short reading and an affirmation waiting. It takes about fifteen seconds and exists mostly to get you into the app while the dream is still fresh — which is the real trick to keeping a dream journal at all.

A streak counter tracks consecutive days.

Also included

Tarot

Single-card readings and a three-card spread for past, present and future, drawn from the 22 Major Arcana with AI-written interpretations. Your draw history is saved.

Horoscopes

Daily and weekly readings, plus love and career variants, for all twelve signs. Each sign also carries its element, ruling planet and compatible signs.

Zodiac compatibility

Pick two signs and see a compatibility score broken into love, communication and passion, with a card you can share.

Numerology

Your Life Path, Expression and Soul Urge numbers from the Pythagorean system, including master numbers.

Manifesto

Write an intention and Pythia turns it into something worth re-reading. Kept alongside your journal as a daily ritual.

Pythia Pro

The app is free to use with a daily allowance of readings. Pro removes the limits: unlimited dream interpretations, every interpretation style, unlimited pattern analysis, and no ads. It starts with a 3-day free trial and can be cancelled at any time from your device settings.
