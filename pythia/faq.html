Using Pythia

How does the interpretation actually work?
You write the dream in free text and an AI model generates an interpretation of what you wrote, in the style you picked. It is not looking your dream up in a table — two people who both dreamt about water will get different readings, because the rest of the dream is different.
Do I have to type? I can barely form sentences when I wake up.
You can dictate instead. Speaking is usually easier first thing in the morning, and the app turns it into text in your journal.
Which interpretation style should I choose?
There is no correct one. Classic is the most neutral starting point. Jungian tends to be the most useful for recurring dreams. Reading the same dream through two styles and comparing them is often more revealing than either on its own.
How many dreams do I need before pattern analysis works?
It needs a handful of interpreted dreams before it has anything meaningful to compare. The app will tell you when you have enough. The more history you build, the more it has to work with.
Do I need an account?
No sign-up form, no password. The app creates an anonymous identity for you so your journal and subscription stay attached to you.
Does it work offline?
Your saved dreams can be read offline, but generating a new interpretation needs a connection, since the AI runs on a server rather than on your phone.

Pro and pricing

Is Pythia free?
Yes, with a daily allowance of readings. You can also earn extra readings by watching an optional ad. Pythia Pro removes the limits entirely.
What does Pro include?
Unlimited dream interpretations, every interpretation style, unlimited pattern analysis, all horoscope readings, the detailed compatibility report, unlimited numerology, and no ads.
How does the free trial work?
Pro starts with a 3-day free trial. If you cancel before it ends you are not charged. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.
How do I cancel?
Through the store you subscribed with, not inside the app. On iOS: Settings, then your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. On Android: the Google Play app, then Payments and subscriptions.

Privacy

Who can read my dreams?
Your journal is tied to your anonymous account and is not shared with third parties or sold. Dreams are sent to an AI provider only for the moment an interpretation is generated. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
Are my dreams used for advertising?
No. Dream content is never used to target ads.
Can I delete my data?
Yes. Individual entries can be deleted in the app, and you can request full deletion by emailing us from the support page.

Devices and languages

Which platforms is Pythia on?
iOS and Android. Get it on the App Store or Google Play.
What languages are supported?
Eight: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Arabic and Hindi. Interpretations are generated in your chosen language, not translated after the fact.
Should I take the interpretations literally?
No. Pythia is for reflection and entertainment. It is not medical, psychological or financial advice, and it does not predict the future. If a dream is connected to something genuinely troubling you, please talk to a qualified professional.
