Subscriptions

Cancelling Pythia Pro

Subscriptions are managed by the app store you bought them from, not inside Pythia — so cancelling has to happen there.

  • iPhone or iPad — Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pythia.
  • Android — open the Google Play app, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the current period ends. To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it finishes.

I paid but Pro is not active

Open the paywall in the app and use Restore Purchases first — that fixes it in most cases, especially after reinstalling or switching phones. If it does not, email us with your store receipt and we will sort it out.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google, not by us, so we cannot issue one directly. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google: the Play Store order history. If something went genuinely wrong on our side, write to us and we will support your request.

Common problems

An interpretation will not generate

Generating a reading needs a connection, since the AI runs on a server. If you are online and it still fails, close the app fully and reopen it. If it keeps happening, tell us roughly when it started — that helps us find it in the logs.

I am out of daily readings

Free accounts have a daily allowance. You can watch an optional ad for an extra reading, or the allowance refreshes the next day. Pythia Pro removes the limit.

Notifications are not arriving

Check that notifications are enabled for Pythia in your device settings. On Android 13 and later, if the permission prompt was declined once it will not appear again, so it has to be turned on manually in system settings.

The app is in the wrong language

Pythia follows your device language across its eight supported languages. If your device is set to a language Pythia does not support, it falls back to English.

Your data

You can delete individual dreams, tarot draws and other entries inside the app at any time. To request deletion of everything associated with your account, email us from the address above with "Data deletion" in the subject line.
