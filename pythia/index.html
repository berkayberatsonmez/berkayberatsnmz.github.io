Pythia

An AI dream journal. Write the dream in your own words and get an interpretation of that dream — not a canned entry from a dictionary.

What Pythia does

Most dream apps are lookup tables. You saw a snake, you get the paragraph about snakes, and everyone who saw a snake gets the same paragraph.

Pythia works the other way around. You write what actually happened — the whole messy thing, in your own words — and the interpretation responds to what you wrote. The setting, who was there, how it felt when you woke up: all of it shapes the reading.

The second half is the journal. Dreams are far more revealing in aggregate than one at a time. Once you have a handful saved, Pythia looks across them and surfaces the symbols that keep returning, how your emotional tone has been trending, and the theme sitting underneath.

Four ways to read a dream

  • Classic symbolic — the traditional reading of images and their long-standing associations.
  • Jungian — archetypes, the shadow, and the parts of yourself a dream might be showing you.
  • Freudian — latent content, displacement, and what the dream might be disguising.
  • Religious and spiritual — interpretation in a faith-oriented frame.

The same dream read four ways rarely gives four similar answers, which is usually the point. See Features for the rest of what is in the app.
