Conveyor Sort

Packages roll down a moving belt. Sort them by colour into bins, seal a bin with three of a kind, and don't let anything slip off the end.

What the game is

Conveyor Sort is a colour-matching puzzle game about keeping up with a belt that never stops. Coloured packages arrive one after another, and a row of bins sits below them. Tap a package to drop it into the matching bin. When a bin holds three packages of the same colour it seals itself, clears away, and a fresh empty bin takes its place.

The pressure comes from the belt. It keeps moving whether you are ready or not, and every package that runs off the end costs you. The puzzle is not which colour goes where — that part is obvious. It is deciding what to do when three colours arrive at once and you only have room for two.

New to it? Start with How to Play, then read the tips and strategy guide.

Features

  • Daily Puzzle — a new challenge every day with a streak to keep alive. Miss a day and the streak resets, so it is worth showing up.
  • Special packages — golden Jokers that fit any bin, wrapped mystery packages that hide their colour until they land, and Bombs that have to be cleared before they go off.
  • Plays offline — no account, no login, no internet connection needed. Open it on a plane and it works exactly the same.
  • Free to play — the whole game is free. Ads that reward you with an extra life or bonus coins are always optional, never forced mid-puzzle.
  • Two languages — full English and Turkish support, picked up from your device settings.
