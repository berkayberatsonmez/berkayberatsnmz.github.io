Before you write: quick fixes

A reward ad will not load

Reward ads are the one part of the game that needs a connection. If nothing loads, check that you are online, then close the game fully and reopen it. Ad availability also varies by region and time of day — sometimes there is simply no ad to serve, and the game will let you carry on without one.

The game is stuttering or dropping frames

Close other apps running in the background and make sure your device is not in a battery saver mode, which caps the frame rate on many Android phones. If it persists, tell us your device model — that is exactly the kind of report that leads to a fix.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored on the device. It is lost if the app's data is cleared, if the game is uninstalled, or when you move to a new phone. There is unfortunately no way to recover it after the fact, since nothing is stored on a server.

The Daily Puzzle is not refreshing

The daily puzzle rolls over on your device's clock. If your date and time are set manually and are wrong, the daily can appear stuck. Switching your phone to automatic date and time resolves it.

Reporting a bug well

The most useful reports answer three things:

  1. What happened — what you saw, in one sentence.
  2. What you expected — sometimes these turn out to be the same thing, which is useful to know too.
  3. How to make it happen again — even a rough guess helps enormously.
