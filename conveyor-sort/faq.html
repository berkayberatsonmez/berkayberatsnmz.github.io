Playing the game

Does Conveyor Sort cost anything?
No. The game is free to download and free to play all the way through. There is no paid unlock and no level gate that requires money to pass.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. The game runs entirely on your device. You can play on a plane, on the underground, or with data switched off, and nothing changes. A connection is only used to load an optional reward ad if you choose to watch one.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login and no profile. Your progress lives on your device.
How do ads work?
Ads are optional and reward-based. You are offered one when you want something in return — an extra attempt, or doubled coins after a win. You can always decline and keep playing. Ads are never inserted into the middle of a puzzle.
What happens when I run out of lives?
The level ends and you are offered the chance to continue instead of starting over. Declining costs you nothing except restarting the level.
I lost my Daily Puzzle streak. Can it be restored?
The streak resets when a day is missed and cannot be restored afterwards. If the day's puzzle beat you rather than you missing it, a second attempt can be unlocked once per day.
What is the golden package with a star?
That is the Joker. It counts as any colour and fits into whichever bin you drop it on. See Tips & Strategy for when it is worth spending.

Devices and languages

Which platforms is it on?
Conveyor Sort is available on Google Play for Android. An iOS version is currently in review with Apple; this site will be updated when it is live.
What languages does it support?
English and Turkish. The game follows your device language automatically.
Will my progress transfer to a new phone?
Progress is stored locally on the device, so it does not follow you to a new phone automatically. Cloud save is not currently part of the game.

Privacy and data

What data does the game collect?
The game does not ask for your name, email or any account details. Anonymous analytics and advertising identifiers are used to keep the game working and to serve ads. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
Why does iOS ask for tracking permission?
On iOS, the advertising identifier is used to choose which ads appear and to measure whether they worked. You can decline the prompt and the game will behave exactly the same — you will simply see less relevant ads.
Is the game suitable for children?
There is no chat, no user-generated content and no way to contact other players. The game does contain advertising. Parents who want an ad-free experience should be aware that ads are shown, though never during a puzzle.
