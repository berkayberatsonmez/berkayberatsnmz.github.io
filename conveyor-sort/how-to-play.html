How to Play

Everything the game does not stop to explain.

The basic loop

  1. Coloured packages travel along a conveyor belt across the screen.
  2. Below the belt sits a row of bins. Each bin has a colour.
  3. Tap a package to send it into the bin of the same colour.
  4. Once a bin holds three packages of its colour, it seals and clears.
  5. A new empty bin opens in its place, in a colour the level still needs.
  6. If a package reaches the end of the belt without being sorted, you lose a life.

That is the whole game. Everything below is a variation on it.

Special packages

Golden Joker

A gold package marked with a star. A Joker counts as any colour, so it fits into whichever bin you drop it on. It is the only piece in the game that can rescue a bin you have no matching colour for — which also makes it the easiest piece to waste. Holding one for a few seconds is often worth more than spending it immediately.

Mystery package

A wrapped package that hides its colour until it lands in a bin. You are committing to a bin before you know what is inside, so mystery packages are best sent to a bin that is nearly empty rather than one sitting on two of three.

Bomb

A package on a timer. Clear it before the timer runs out. Bombs are the one piece you cannot leave for later, so when one appears it takes priority over almost any other plan you had.

Lives and continuing

You start each level with a set number of lives. Every package that falls off the end of the belt costs one. Lose them all and the level ends.

When a level ends you are offered the chance to continue rather than start over. Taking it is entirely your choice — declining simply restarts the level with nothing lost but time.

The Daily Puzzle

Once a day the game offers a fixed puzzle that is the same for everyone playing that day. Clearing it extends your streak; missing a day resets the streak to zero.

The Daily Puzzle behaves a little differently from the normal levels: it is a single attempt rather than something you can restart freely from the pause menu. If the day's puzzle beats you, a second attempt can be unlocked once per day.

Coins

Finishing levels earns coins. After a win you may be offered the chance to double the coins you just earned. Coins accumulate on your device and are never required to keep playing — the full game is completable without spending any.
