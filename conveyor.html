Conveyor Sort

Sort colorful packages by color as they flow down a moving conveyor belt. Fill a bin with three of the same color to seal it — but don't let packages slip off the end!

Features

  • Daily Puzzle — a new hand-crafted challenge every day, with a streak to keep alive.
  • Special packages — golden Jokers that fit any bin, wrapped mystery packages, and ticking Bombs.
  • Plays offline — no account, no login, no internet required to play.
  • Free with optional ads — watch-to-earn rewards are entirely optional.
