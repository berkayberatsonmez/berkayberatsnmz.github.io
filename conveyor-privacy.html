Privacy Policy

Conveyor Sort  ·  Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Conveyor Sort mobile game (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you play. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

No account, no personal information

The App does not require registration or login. We do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Information collected automatically

To display ads and understand how the App is used, the following may be collected automatically:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / Advertising ID on Android)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • In-app usage events (e.g. levels started/completed, features used)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (country/region level, not precise GPS)

Third-party services

The App uses trusted third-party services that may process the data above under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — AdMob & Google's use of data and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics (usage analytics) — see Firebase Privacy & Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok integration

Conveyor Sort offers an optional tool to publish short gameplay clips to a TikTok account that you connect and authorize. When you connect a TikTok account, we use TikTok's Content Posting API solely to publish content that you explicitly choose to post. We request only the permissions needed to identify the connected account and to post on its behalf. We do not read your TikTok activity or private data, and we do not store your TikTok content. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before using your advertising identifier for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads (non-personalized), and ad measurement relies on Apple's SKAdNetwork.

Local storage on your device

Your game progress and settings (unlocked levels, coins, preferences) are stored locally on your device. We operate no game servers for this data, and it is not uploaded to us.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will address it.

Data security & changes

We rely on the security measures of the third-party services listed above. This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time; material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
